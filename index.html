About Me

Speedrunning around real life.

By day I'm a licensed therapist in Minnesota. Outside of that, I'm a husband, a dad, and someone who squeezes in speedruns, marathons, and the occasional stream whenever life gives me the chance.

Outside of work, I spend time with my wife Savana, our two boys, and our dogs Ivy and Fynn, and whenever I can, I like to put some of that leftover energy into speedrunning.

I'm pretty open, so if you ever have a question about me, the stream, or a run, feel free to ask. This site is really just my way of keeping the speedrunning side of life organized without losing the personal side of it.
